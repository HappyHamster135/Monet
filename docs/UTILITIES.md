# Monet Utilities on 0.9.8-9

Open **Monet Utilities** from the application menu. Type in the search field to find a tool. Press **Super+Alt+Space** to open the tool search with your favorites. Type a name such as “volume mixer” or “scratchpad”, then press Enter to use it. The Utilities results also appear in the Home Bar and in desktop search (**Super+R**). Type **tools** to list favorites. The search provider respects hidden tools. The gear lets you hide tools and choose up to eight favorites. **Ctrl+K** returns to search from any tool. The launcher's right-click menu also opens the shelf, scratchpad and favorites wheel or switches audio outputs.

In **Customize Home Bar → Top-center notch**, select **Compact** or **Wide**. The notch belongs to the screen containing the bar you customized. Moving the Home Bar leaves the notch at the top. Select **Off** to remove it. Music levels, notification previews, the companion and low accessory battery alerts each have a separate control.

- Click the notch to open Music, Activity, Time, Calendar or Notes. Scroll down over the notch to open it and up to close it. A horizontal wheel or trackpad scroll over it skips a track when the player supports that action.
- In Music, **Lyrics** follows playback when the player shares timed words or a local song has a matching `.lrc` file. Select a line to seek in a supported player. **Up next** shows the next tracks from players that share their queue.
- Open **Music → Mixer** to adjust an app’s volume or mute it. An app with more than one playing stream has one row. Its **⋯ → Play through** menu lets you send it to a different output while other apps keep playing where they are. Pin an app to keep its row when playback stops. Drag an app’s name or choose **Move up** or **Move down** to reorder it within its group. Settings → Sound and the Home Bar sound popup share these controls and preferences.
- Drop files on the notch, then drag them from **File shelf** into another application. Removing an entry from the shelf keeps the original file.
- In **Activity → Notifications**, choose a message to read it, open the sender’s action, reply when supported, or dismiss it from desktop history. **Clear notifications…** asks for confirmation and keeps messages that arrive or change while you decide. For five-second previews on the closed notch, enable **Preview notifications in the notch** in Customize Home Bar. Click a preview to read that message. Previews respect the sender’s popup preference, Do Not Disturb and the screen lock.
- Open **Activity → Downloads** for the twelve most recent files in the watched folder. Partial downloads are labeled. Finished files have **Show in Files** and **Add to file shelf** actions. Transfers that report progress to KDE can show it here. **Folder…** lets you choose a different location or return to your Downloads folder.
- A focus timer borrows Monet's quiet mode until its deadline. The stopwatch and countdown keep their state when the popup closes. Calendar uses the same day notes as the Monet clock.
- In **Notes**, choose a pad or create one with **New pad**. Type directly in the notch, or select **Open floating pad** to keep the note above another app. **Utilities → Scratchpad → Pad options** lets you rename a pad, preview Markdown, export a copy, or delete it. Your previous scratchpad becomes the first pad. If two editors change the same text at once, both versions are saved.
- In **Calendar → Upcoming appointments**, browse two weeks at a time. **Calendars…** lets you link a local iCalendar (.ics) file or subscribe with an HTTPS or webcal link. File edits appear when you reopen or refresh the agenda. Subscriptions keep their last successful copy for offline use. Remove a calendar here to clear its cached copy and keep the original.
- In **Activity → Watch**, choose a window in the desktop sharing picker. Drag over the part you want to follow and select **Show in notch**. Click the notch to see the live region. **Edit region and alerts** lets you change the selection, watch for a phrase, or get one alert when the picture changes or settles. **Stop watching** ends sharing. You can also use Watch as a separate Utilities window.
- In **Settings → Sound → Preferred output order**, choose **Manage…**, add your devices, put the first choice at the top and select **Apply**. Disconnected devices keep their place. A manual output selection lasts until the next connection change. Turn off **Switch outputs automatically** to keep the list for later. The launcher’s **Cycle sound output** action switches directly and names the output that took over.
- In **Settings → Bluetooth**, enable **Turn Bluetooth off during sleep** to disconnect accessories when the computer sleeps. **Restore Bluetooth on wake** turns the same adapters back on and reconnects previously connected mice and keyboards. Switch it off to leave Bluetooth off after waking.
- Open **Screen recorder and editor** in Utilities. Choose **Record…** to select a region, window or screen. The same menu lets you include system sound or your microphone. These choices are shared with Spectacle. Click the recording indicator in the tray to finish and open the take. **Open recording…** edits an existing video. Set the start and end, draw a crop, choose playback speed or mute audio. Under **Overlays**, add a caption, place a local image or blur an area. Drag the box to move it and its corner to resize it. Set when it appears and disappears. Use **Bring forward** or **Send backward** to change which overlay is on top. Select an existing overlay from the menu to edit or remove it. **Export copy…** saves a new MP4 or looping GIF.
- In **Listening ports**, search by port, address, protocol, process name, or PID. **Manage process…** shows the owner and its children. Choose a process and select **End process…** or **Force quit…**. Turn on **Include child processes** to act on the displayed branch. **End or restart a process** also finds processes without a listening port. **Restart app…** reopens a recognized desktop app after its original process exits.
- Open **Homebrew packages** to see what you installed, filter dependencies or find updates. In **Find packages**, choose Formulae or Casks, type a name and press Enter. Select a result for details or to install it. **Update Homebrew** refreshes the package catalog. **Update all…** upgrades eligible packages and respects pins. If Monet cannot find a working Homebrew installation, open the setup guide or choose an executable under **Options**.
- Open **Cleaning mode**, choose 30 seconds, one minute or two minutes, then **Start cleaning**. A dark screen keeps keyboard and pointer input away from your apps and the password field. Click **Finish cleaning** or press Enter five times to finish early. When the timer ends, unlock your session as usual. The physical power and brightness controls still belong to the system.
- In **Quit and close protection**, choose **Quit application** for Ctrl+Q or **Close tab or window** for Ctrl+W. Enable either shortcut and choose a hold, a second press, or an extra key to confirm it. You can adjust the timing and include or exclude individual apps. Both protections start off.
- Press **Super+Ctrl+V** in an editable field to paste without formatting. Your next normal paste can still use the original styling. To change the keys, open **Paste as plain text shortcut** in Utilities and search for **Paste as plain text**.
- In **Copied link settings**, turn on **Clean copied links automatically**. Copy a plain web address and paste it in any app. Monet removes common tracking parameters while preserving the remaining query and fragment. You can also use **Clean a copied link** once.
- In **AI session usage → Account limits**, turn on **Show Codex account limits** to see how much of each Codex window is left and its local reset time. Activity in the notch shows the same figures. The signed-in Codex CLI checks its service on demand. **Refresh** requests a new reading. **Choose Codex CLI…** lets you select a portable installation. Turning the switch off clears the saved reading. The separate **Session file** tab reads usage from a local log or database you choose. **Forget** clears that selection.

## Coverage of the reference app

The product reference is [Vorssaint’s feature list](https://vorssaint.com/). Its macOS utilities and Monet’s Linux desktop have different system interfaces. This table records the scope of the local build.

| Area | Available in Monet | Remaining differences |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Windows and taskbar | Existing switcher, window previews, taskbar interactions, maximize and snap layouts. The title controls follow a stationary pointer after focus changes and stay available as the snap preview fades. | A selectable quit-on-last-window rule for individual apps. |
| Mouse and keyboard | KDE mouse, trackpad, focus and keyboard settings. Input Remapper provides button, key and modifier mappings. Keyboard bounce and sticky-key settings are available. Quit and close shortcut protection has independent confirmation methods, timing and application lists. | Dedicated smooth and linear wheel profiles and per-button extra-click filtering. Three-finger click availability depends on the device and its settings. |
| Clipboard and files | Clipboard history, copying as plain text, pasting without formatting with Super+Ctrl+V, manual or automatic link cleanup, reusable snippets, file cut/paste and rename in Files, shelf and download folder monitoring. | Automatic text-trigger expansion. Monet uses Linux app installation formats. |
| Audio, energy and displays | Per-app volumes and devices in Monet Sound, automatic output priority, cycle output, microphone mute, timed keep-awake, optional Bluetooth power and reconnection on wake, brightness and hardware-supported HDR. | Per-player blocking of media keys. Apple XDR brightness is hardware-specific. |
| Desktop tools | Search, favorites, wheel, named floating scratchpads with Markdown preview and text export, wallpaper picker, camera mirror, color picker, local text/QR recognition, screenshots, screen recording, trimming, cropping, speed, timed captions, image overlays, blur regions and MP4/GIF export, and media compression. Storage and Apps handle cleanup and removal. Timed cleaning mode covers the locked displays and returns to the normal unlock screen. Searchable listening ports and process trees support quit, force quit and relaunching recognized desktop apps. Homebrew package search, installation, updates and removal work with an existing installation. Task Manager and Updates open on demand. | Cleaning with the desktop visible and automatic recording zoom. |
| Notch | Media playback, synchronized and plain lyrics, live player queues, a per-app mixer with output routing and pinned apps, real output levels, editable scratchpads, calendar, day notes and upcoming appointments from iCalendar files and subscriptions, notification actions and replies, optional short notification previews, timers, accessory levels and low-battery alerts, recent downloads with file actions and native transfer progress, local agent usage, opt-in Codex account limits with reset times, live views of selected window regions with change and text alerts, and an optional companion. | Live account limits for other providers. |
| System monitoring | Existing Task Manager views cover CPU, graphics, memory, network and disks. Utilities also exposes USB devices, power and readable fan sensors. | Manual fan curves require a supported controller and a separate compatible control service. The ThinkPad test covers fan readout only when the firmware exposes it. |
